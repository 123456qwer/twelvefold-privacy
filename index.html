隐私政策

本政策说明 player_wd（GitHub：123456qwer）开发的 Twelvefold / 十二庭如何处理游戏数据，以及本政策网页的托管方式。

1. 离线游戏与账户

游戏的基本游玩功能可离线使用，无需注册游戏账户。游戏不内置广告、广告跟踪或第三方广告、行为分析 SDK，也不会将你的游戏存档、偏好设置或图形缓存上传到开发者运营的服务器。

2. 设备上的本地数据

为保存进度、记住设置并正常运行，游戏在设备本地处理以下信息：

  • 存档：游戏进度、角色状态、装备、任务、角色关系及存档创建／最近游玩时间等。
  • 偏好：语言、摇杆模式与位置、操作按钮布局等设置。
  • 运行与图形缓存：系统版本、引擎和图形环境等信息在本地用于生成缓存指纹及着色器缓存，减少重复准备时间。

这些信息用于游戏功能和性能，不用于广告画像或跨应用跟踪。

3. Apple 内购与购买权限

在启用内购功能的版本中，购买及恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。游戏会读取商品信息、Apple 已验证的购买状态和交易标识，以解锁或恢复内容，并可在本地保存加密的购买权限缓存。游戏不会获取或存储你的付款卡信息或 Apple 账户密码。

购买缓存可使用平台提供的本机设备标识派生加密密钥。该标识仅在设备本地使用，不上传到开发者服务器，也不用于广告或跨应用跟踪。普通存档和偏好文件不因此被视为全部加密。

购买、恢复及商店验证可能需要联网。未启用内购的版本可能不执行上述商店连接和购买缓存处理。

4. 数据共享与广告

我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不会将其提供给广告商或用于广告定向。游戏没有自行向开发者发送游玩遥测的功能。Apple 平台服务、本页的 GitHub 托管，以及你主动发送的支持邮件，分别按下文所述处理。

5. Apple 平台服务、诊断与反馈

Apple 可在 App Store、TestFlight 和操作系统服务中处理购买、崩溃或性能诊断，以及你主动提交的测试反馈。根据平台功能和你的设置，Apple 也可能向开发者提供相关报告或反馈。这些处理由 Apple 的政策及平台设置管理，不等同于游戏内置第三方跟踪。

详情见 Apple 隐私政策。

6. 本地数据、备份与删除

游戏没有自己的云存档或开发者云同步服务。操作系统、设备迁移或你选择的备份服务可能保存本地应用数据的副本，具体取决于平台及你的设置。

本地数据在保留于设备期间用于继续游戏和记住设置。你可在游戏内重新开始并确认覆盖相应存档；这只重置该存档，不会删除全部偏好、缓存或 Apple 购买记录。其他本地数据可通过平台支持的清除应用数据或卸载方式处理，实际删除效果及备份保留取决于平台。删除本地文件不会删除 Apple 持有的交易记录。

7. 主动联系、保留与数据请求

如果你通过邮件联系我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你选择附加的信息。我们仅将这些信息用于回复、排查问题、后续沟通和处理隐私请求，并在完成这些用途及履行适用义务所必要的期限内保留；不再需要时删除或去除可识别信息。

你可使用下方联系邮箱，请求查询、更正或删除主动提供给我们的个人信息。我们可能需要合理核实请求与有关信息的关联。设备本地文件由你通过游戏及系统管理；Apple 交易、平台报告或备份中的资料，还需依相应平台的流程处理。

8. 儿童信息

我们不会主动向儿童索取个人信息。如果你认为儿童通过支持邮件等方式向我们发送了个人信息，请联系下方邮箱，我们会核查并处理删除请求。

9. 本政策网页的托管

本政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。本页面不加载外部字体、图片、脚本或分析工具，不设置自己的 Cookie，也没有表单。为提供托管服务和保障安全，GitHub 可能记录访问者的 IP 地址、浏览器或请求信息等；这与游戏自身的数据处理分开。

详情见 GitHub 一般隐私声明。

10. 政策更新

如果数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在本页公布更新并修改生效日期。更新后的政策自标注的生效日期起适用。本次生效及更新日期为 2026-11-05。

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Twelvefold / 十二庭, developed by player_wd (GitHub: 123456qwer), handles game data and how this policy webpage is hosted.

1. Offline play and accounts

The core game can be played offline without registering a game account. The game does not include advertising, advertising tracking, or third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. It does not upload your game saves, preferences, or graphics caches to developer-operated servers.

2. Data on your device

To save progress, remember settings, and run the game, the app processes the following information locally on your device:

  • Save data: game progress, character state, equipment, quests, character relationships, and save creation or last-played timestamps.
  • Preferences: language, joystick mode and position, control-button layout, and related settings.
  • Runtime and graphics caches: system version, engine, and graphics-environment information is used locally to generate cache fingerprints and shader caches, reducing repeated preparation.

This information supports gameplay and performance. It is not used for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking.

3. Apple purchases and access

In versions where in-app purchases are enabled, Apple StoreKit handles purchases and restoration. The app reads product information, Apple-verified purchase status, and transaction identifiers to unlock or restore content. It may store an encrypted entitlement cache locally. The app does not receive or store your payment-card information or Apple Account password.

A platform-provided device identifier may be used locally to derive the purchase cache's encryption key. It is not uploaded to developer-operated servers or used for advertising or cross-app tracking. This does not mean that ordinary save and preference files are all encrypted.

Purchases, restoration, and store verification may require an internet connection. Versions without enabled purchases may not perform this store connection or purchase-cache processing.

4. Sharing and advertising

We do not sell your personal information or provide it to advertisers or use it for targeted advertising. The game does not independently send gameplay telemetry to the developer. Apple platform services, GitHub hosting for this page, and support emails you choose to send are handled separately as described below.

5. Apple services, diagnostics, and feedback

Apple may process purchases, crash or performance diagnostics, and feedback you choose to submit through the App Store, TestFlight, or operating-system services. Depending on platform features and your settings, Apple may also provide related reports or feedback to the developer. Apple's policies and platform settings govern this processing; it is separate from third-party tracking within the game.

See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Local data, backups, and deletion

The game does not operate its own cloud-save or developer cloud-sync service. Your operating system, device migration, or backup services you choose may retain copies of local app data, depending on the platform and your settings.

Local data supports continued play and remembered settings while it remains on your device. Starting a new game and confirming that a save is overwritten resets that save only; it does not delete all preferences, caches, or Apple purchase records. Other local data can be managed through platform-supported app-data removal or uninstallation. The actual deletion and retention of backups depend on the platform. Removing local files does not delete transaction records held by Apple.

7. Support, retention, and data requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to attach. We use this information only to reply, investigate issues, follow up, and handle privacy requests. We retain it for as long as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations, then delete it or remove identifying information when it is no longer needed.

You can use the contact address below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have voluntarily provided to us. We may reasonably verify that a request relates to the information concerned. You manage files on your device through the game and operating system. Information in Apple transactions, platform reports, or backups may also need to be managed through the relevant platform's processes.

8. Children's information

We do not actively request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, please contact us below so we can review and address a deletion request.

9. Hosting of this policy webpage

This policy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages. The page does not load external fonts, images, scripts, or analytics tools, set its own cookies, or include forms. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses, browser or request information, and similar information to provide and secure its hosting service. This is separate from the game's data processing.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Policy updates

If our data practices change, we will publish an update here and revise the effective date. An updated policy applies from its stated effective date. This policy is effective and was last updated on November 5, 2026.
